Idols

Meet the artists who inspire us beyond the beat.

Kim Seung-min

Singer • MC

South Korean singer and MC, best known as the main vocalist of the boy group Stray Kids under JYP Entertainment.

Daniela Avanzini

Singer • Dancer

An American singer and dancer best known as a member of the global girl group Katseye, formed via HYBE and Geffen Records’ 2023 reality show

IU

Vocalist • Performer

Lee Ji-eun, known by her stage name IU, is a South Korean singer-songwriter, actress, and philanthropist. Debuting at age 15 with the EP Lost and Found (2008), she rose to fame with the hit Good Day (2010), ranked by Billboard as the top K-pop song of the 2010s.

Jung Kook

Singer • Dancer

Jeon Jung-kook, known professionally as Jung Kook, is a South Korean singer and songwriter who rose to prominence as the youngest member and main vocalist of BTS.

Jeongyeon

Artist • Performer

Yu Jeong-yeon (Korean: 유정연; born 1 November 1996), known mononymously as Jeongyeon, is a South Korean singer. She is a member of Twice, a South Korean girl group formed by JYP Entertainment.

Wonhee

Singer • Performer

Lee Won-hee (Korean: 이원희; born June 26, 2007), known mononymously as Wonhee, is a South Korean singer. She is a member of the girl group Illit under Belift Lab and Hybe, formed through the 2023 survival show R U Next?.
